# Radius snapshot review (2026-09-27)

This branch derives a new snapshot from package 0.4.0; the release 0.4.0 is immutable. No registry-site database, full-grid expansion, release, or deployment is included.

- 702 stored directed records; 19 are now labelled `paper_proved_exact` with exact values and written manuscript theorem references (Theorems 4.3–4.8). Of those, eight have separately bundled replayable certificates; the other eleven do **not** become machine-certified by changing a label. Crescent→exponential is `sin(1)` (Theorem 4.4(a)).
- New status counts: 19 paper-proved, 306 touch-proven (old snapshot label), 139 closed-form-confirmed, 84 unidentified, 142 trivial, 12 quarantined. These are raw, alias-inclusive records. The six previously missed real-axis cases retain `unidentified` because the axis equation identifies contact but does not establish global containment. Their exact implicit equations are stored as `axis_equation`; five do not have a supported explicit `value_exact` grammar (parabolic→rational_kr already does).
- The diagnostic bisection of φ₁(±r)=φ₂(±1) matches all 409 stored real-axis decimal radii within 1e-6. This is a numerical cross-check, **not** a proof of sharpness or a correction of the 12 `audit_required` records. All twelve quarantined values also match the axis equation numerically; ten have target `janowski_A0.75_B-0.25`. Their failed symbolic consistency checks require investigation in the upstream registry, not blind promotion.
- The `janowski_A0_B-1` generator duplicates `order_0.5`. Both public keys and raw radius records remain available; the canonical *analysis* merges this alias.
